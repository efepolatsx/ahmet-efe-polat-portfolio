01 / ABOUT

I am an architecture student interested in how people experience space — from the scale of a city block to the details of a room.

Based in Istanbul, I use drawing, modelling, and visual research to explore context, movement, material, and atmosphere. I approach each project as an opportunity to question familiar spatial conditions and propose thoughtful alternatives.
